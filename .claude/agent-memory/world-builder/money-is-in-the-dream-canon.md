On 2026-07-10, established lore for the 4-year-skip beat where Aria's tattoo triggers Adrian's firing from NMPD:

- **Tattoo Phenomenon**: NOT a curse or pure genetic trait. Ruled as a latent cultivation-affinity mark — dormant qi left over from Tajanda's dimensional rift (see existing [[tajanda]] canon) that surfaces in some individuals, especially those with proximity/sensitivity to dream energy, typically emerging young. Flagged citywide via mandatory anomaly reporting statute administered out of the Aequorium (existing "aequorium" location page). This slots the tattoo into the existing Tajanda/rift magic system rather than inventing a parallel one. **Ongoing-danger clarification (2026-07-11):** the mark is permanent — it does not fade once Aria leaves city jurisdiction, because it reflects a real latent affinity, not an externally-applied curse. The danger was never supernatural/personal (it will not hurt her, curse her, or actively attract Dream-World attention on its own); the danger is entirely the bureaucratic exposure — being inside a system with mandatory-reporting statutes. Once she is outside the city's institutional oversight (registry, precincts, licensed medical reporting), the classification has no enforcement reach and she is functionally safe. Residual risk: if she or Adrian ever re-enters a jurisdiction with an equivalent reporting regime, the same exposure could recur — this keeps a story hook alive without contradicting "she's safe now."
- **Adrian's captain backstory**: Existing "Adrian (Light)" character page (id `adrian`, content.json) describes him ONLY as a late-20s lighting/rigging specialist on the Dreamcatchers crew, with an explicit OPEN "backstory specifics" field. Ruled: the NMPD captain history is prior backstory — Adrian was captain before the beat's firing, and the firing is *why* he ended up doing lighting/rigging work by the time of the crew's present-day timeline. This reconciles cleanly rather than contradicting; the existing page's "protective," "stoic," "matter-of-fact resistance to supernatural influence" traits are direct throughlines from the captain years covering for Aria. **Cross-reference (2026-07-11):** the firing ends his captaincy and all official authority immediately — his ability to actually retrieve Aria from the orphanage afterward is NOT via rank or procedure; see [[Adrian's Custody of Aria]] entry below for the documented (informal, favor-based) method. Do not assume he had any automatic post-firing institutional access.
- **NMPD structure**: Precinct = existing "Waterfront Police Precinct (海滨大道警察局)" location page (id `waterfront-precinct`) — a 6-floor Second Empire/Haussmann admin building on Seaside Boulevard. Ruled this is Adrian's precinct/HQ.
- **New NPC — Chief Elias Thorne**: District police chief, Adrian's superior, the one who fired him. Designed as a recurring antagonist figure representing institutional/bureaucratic suppression of anomalies — a thematic mirror/foil to Arthur Crowe (existing NPC, id `arthur-crowe`), who protects supernatural secrets benevolently while Thorne enforces suppression through paperwork and policy. Open for narrative-director to decide if/how he recurs.
- **New Miri Orphanage**: New location/institution, not yet in content.json. Ruled it has a standing mandatory-reporting relationship with city medical/police systems (routes through the Aequorium's civil registry), which is why Aria's doctor's report reached official channels at all and why the orphanage has procedural authority to hand her over/remove her.
- **Adrian's Custody of Aria (2026-07-11, ESTABLISHED CANON):** Adrian has no legal captain authority left by the time he extracts Aria — the firing precedes the extraction. Ruled Option A: Adrian has a sympathetic staff contact inside New Miri Orphanage (an orderly or intake clerk who has quietly covered for Aria before, e.g. delaying the mandatory-report paperwork once already) who facilitates an informal, undocumented handoff — no custody transfer paperwork exists. This is opportunistic, not premeditated: Adrian doesn't forge documents or use rank; he calls in a personal favor from someone already sympathetic to the case, consistent with him having covered for Aria before as captain. It also deliberately leaves a loose thread: the orphanage's official ledger will show Aria as unaccounted-for, not formally released, which is a live investigative hook for Chief Thorne later.

**Why**: Beat needed to explain (a) what makes the tattoo dangerous within existing rules, (b) how a firing over one buried report is structurally plausible, (c) who did the firing, (d) why an orphanage can trigger a police-medical anomaly flag. Grounding the tattoo in the Tajanda rift (rather than inventing new magic) keeps the world's supernatural system singular and avoids contradiction.

**How to apply**: Future lore touching NMPD, the Waterfront Precinct, Aria's tattoo, the Aequorium's civil/administrative authority, or Chief Thorne should stay consistent with this.
